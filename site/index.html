The hidden cost of unfinished mental loops

We often mistake the feeling of being 'busy' for the state of being productive. In reality, much of our exhaustion comes from the mental overhead of unfinished loops. Every half-started project, every deferred decision, and every vague intention we keep in the back of our minds acts like a background application draining a battery. It isn’t always the size of the work that wears us down; it’s the persistent, low-level friction of knowing something is left incomplete. Growth isn’t always about adding more to your plate. Sometimes, it is about closing the loops that remain open. Clear the queue, make the final decision, or consciously choose to discard the task entirely. Stop carrying the weight of things you aren’t actually doing.
